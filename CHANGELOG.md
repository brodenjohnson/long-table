# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (playtest)
The first playable build of the new game.

- An isometric valley: the farmhouse, a field with a poisoned east corner, the well, the depot by the road, a creek, pines, ruins and the ash flats. Every picture is drawn by the game's own code.
- Farming: turn soil, plant eight crops across four seasons (Wet, Green, Dry, Cold), water every day or they die. Fertility and care set quality (Fair, Good, Prime). Crops in bad ground come out tainted. Sunflowers draw the poison out of the soil around them. Compost bins turn fibre and tainted produce into compost.
- Weather: rain waters the field, dust storms dry crops out, frost stops tender crops growing. The radio gives tomorrow's forecast.
- The Hull truck comes on Tuesdays and Fridays. Buy seed and supplies from Ines Varga, sell to her directly, or leave produce in the depot crate and it is paid for when the truck leaves at five. Selling a lot of one thing lowers its price for a few days.
- Hired hands turn up at the gate. They water and harvest and carry produce to the crate, or stand guard at night. They want a wage and a bunk. Treat them badly and they walk, and take something with them.
- Husks come at night from the ruins, drawn to light and noise, and trample crops. Fight them with the machete or the pistol (every shot is heard). Fences, gates, lamps and spike traps. If you sleep, the night still happens: traps, guards and fences decide how much is lost.
- The Sallow Crew: once the farm is worth something, their man asks for a tithe. Pay in scrip or produce, refuse and they come for the crate at ten with torches, or strike him and start it now.
- Cooking at the campfire. Five papers to find around the valley. A map (M). A journal with goals, and the next one on the HUD.
- Hens: buy them from the truck, keep them in a coop. They forage by day, lay if they ate, and are at risk if Husks reach an open farm.
- Fourteen achievements, kept ready to forward to Steam.
- Peaceful, Normal and Hard. Gore can be turned down or off. Rebindable keys, controller support, text size, zoom, screen shake.
- Saves: three slots, autosave every morning and every 90 seconds, export and import. Version 2 save format with migrations.
- Sound: effects, wind, rain, creek, fire and crickets, and a generative plucked-string score, all synthesised.
